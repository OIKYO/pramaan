# Terms of use

**Last changed: 8 November 2026**

These terms explain the conditions for using Pramaan during its pilot.

Pramaan is a poster-checking service built and run by team HEISEN-DE-BUG (Oikyo Chowdhury, Rishav Nandi, Rudrapriyo Chatterjee and Zihan Sirat), based in India, for CodeNexus India 2026. It helps organisations check the posters made by their branches against records and rulebooks approved by head office. It is not yet operated by a registered company. These terms are governed by the laws of India.

## 1. What Pramaan does

Pramaan reads uploaded poster images and captions, checks their content against an organisation's rulebooks and marks problems with explanations and suggested fixes.

It supports a review process involving branch staff, regional managers and head office. The organisation remains responsible for deciding whether a poster should be published.

Pramaan does not design, edit or publish posters. It does not approve posters. It is not legal advice, and passing every check does not guarantee that a poster is lawful.

## 2. Acceptable use

When using Pramaan, you agree to:

- Upload only posters and other material that you have the right to use.
- Obtain appropriate signed consent before adding people to the organisation's register or showing them in publicity.
- Follow applicable laws and the terms of the services you use.
- Provide accurate information when maintaining approved records and rulebooks.
- Avoid uploading real personal data during the pilot.

Do not use Pramaan to upload material that you are not authorised to use or to bypass another person's consent, and do not try to reach another organisation's information or disrupt the service.

## 3. Accounts and passwords

Users must provide the information required to create and use an account: a name, a work email and a password.

The organisation password is created when an organisation is set up. It cannot be recovered if forgotten. Supabase handles sign-in and stores passwords in hashed form. Passwords must have at least eight characters, including a number and a special character. Keep your password to yourself.

An organisation is responsible for managing its team, roles and access. Head office can remove a person's access immediately, while their history remains in the audit log.

## 4. Reviews and approvals

Branch staff, regional managers and head office have different responsibilities within the organisation.

Nobody can approve a poster they made. Approval is refused while a blocking problem remains open. Only head office can override a block, and it must record a written reason.

The organisation is responsible for its final publication decisions. AI readings, warnings and check results do not replace human review.

## 5. Pilot availability

Pramaan is free during the pilot. There are no paid plans at present.

The service is a pilot and may change, become temporarily unavailable or stop. No uptime guarantee, service-level agreement or continued availability is promised. If the pilot ends, we'll tell head offices in Pramaan before deleting organisations' information.

## 6. Data and permissions

You are responsible for ensuring that the material and information you upload may be used for the purposes of checking and managing your organisation's posters.

During the pilot, Pramaan uses free AI tiers. Google may use free-tier inputs to improve its models. For this reason, you must not upload real personal data during the pilot.

Head office can export the organisation's records as CSV files from each rulebook page and the Team page. How long information is kept, and how it is deleted, is described in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy).

## 7. Ending access

- Head office can remove a team member's access at once from the Team page. Their history remains in the audit log.
- Anyone can delete their own account from Settings. An organisation's only head office must first make someone else head office, or delete the organisation.
- Head office can close the organisation by deleting it from Organisation › Profile. Its information is deleted at once and can't be recovered.

We may suspend access that breaks these terms.

## 8. Changes to these terms

When these terms change, we'll update the date at the top and say so in Pramaan before the change takes effect. Using Pramaan after that means you accept the new terms.

## 9. Contact

For questions or issues, use the [Pramaan GitHub issues page](https://github.com/OIKYO/pramaan/issues). For anything private, open an issue saying you need to reach us privately, without personal details, and the team will reply.
